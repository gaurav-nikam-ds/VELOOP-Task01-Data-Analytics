17 · Controls and accountability

Privacy & data governance

A proposed operating model for protecting user data and keeping metric definitions accountable.

Access by role

  • Product: aggregated product performance
  • Analytics: pseudonymous event-level data
  • Finance: restricted ledger and payout detail
  • Engineering: operational access by need

Data minimisation

  • Collect only fields tied to documented purpose
  • Mask or pseudonymise direct identifiers
  • Restrict sensitive exports
  • Define retention and deletion schedule

Ownership

  • Product owns event meaning and eligibility
  • Engineering owns instrumentation and reliability
  • Finance / Ops own ledger and payout definitions
  • Analytics owns metric logic and monitoring

Change management

  • Version schemas and KPI definitions
  • Record approver, reason and effective date
  • Test breaking changes and backfills
  • Maintain access and audit logs
This is a proposed governance framework, not a legal compliance certification. Legal and Security must review actual purpose, jurisdiction, lawful basis, retention and vendor controls.
